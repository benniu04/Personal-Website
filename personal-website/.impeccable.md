# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and fellow builders evaluating Ben Niu — a CS & Finance student at
Northeastern building full-stack products and exploring fintech. They skim quickly on desktop or
phone, looking for proof of craft, shipped work, and taste.

### Brand Personality
Warm, editorial, composed. The site should feel like a well-set print piece: calm confidence,
generous whitespace, zero gimmicks. Emotional goal: "this person sweats the details."

### Aesthetic Direction
Modeled directly on tennr.com (user-requested reference):
- **Palette**: warm cream/blush surfaces (#F6EBE0 / #F1DFD1), espresso ink (#43261A / #5F4037),
  burnt-rust accent (#A24A21 → #7E3616 gradients), linen text on dark (#EEDFD1), cocoa footer (#5F4037).
- **Type**: light serif display in sentence case (Sentient ≈ Feature Display Light), neutral warm
  grotesque body (Schibsted Grotesk ≈ ABC Marfa), mono eyebrow labels (Spline Sans Mono ≈ Marfa Mono)
  set 12–13px uppercase with ~0.18em tracking and middle-dot separators.
- **Signature motif**: stepped "pixel pyramid" — grid of squares stepping through rust shades by
  distance, used in the hero and footer.
- **Buttons**: square corners (0 radius), small uppercase tracked labels; solid espresso/linen and
  thin-outline variants.
- **Layout**: full-bleed color bands, eyebrow + large serif heading per section, editorial rows with
  hairline rules instead of card-everything, flat white cards with 1px borders for projects.
- Anti-references: dark "AI portfolio" with neon/cyan glow, glassmorphism, 3D tilt cards, gradient text.

### Design Principles
1. Print-like calm: cream paper, espresso ink, one rust accent — never more.
2. Serif speaks, mono labels, sans explains. Three voices, strict roles.
3. Hairline rules and whitespace create structure; cards only where content is truly card-shaped.
4. Square corners and tracked caps give the quiet industrial edge.
5. Motion is rare and soft: fades and small rises, exponential ease-out only.
