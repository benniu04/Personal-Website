# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers and fellow builders evaluating Ben Niu, a CS & Finance student at
Northeastern building full-stack products and exploring fintech. Most will skim on a laptop or a
phone for under a minute; every fact has to be reachable by plain scrolling.

### Concept
**The island.** One page. A small low-poly island sits behind the text, and scrolling carries the
camera around it: arrival from the sea at dawn, the cabin (about), the lighthouse path
(experience), the workshop huts (projects, toolkit, commit graph), the clearing (piano, tennis,
golf, gym) and the summit campfire at dusk (contact). Each stop is a card of real HTML; the stretch
between cards is left empty so the island is in full view while the camera travels.

### Brand Personality
Calm, light, considered. A quiet place rather than a game: "this person has taste, sweats the
details, and doesn't need to shout."

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Palette** (tokens in `tailwind.config.js`): sky `#F3F1EC`, paper `#FFFFFF`, ink `#16191C`,
  muted `#566065`, line `#E4E2DB`, and one accent, coral `#C4441F` (as paint in the scene:
  `#E2765A`). Scene colours live in `src/island/palette.js`: pale sea, muted greens, sand,
  off-white walls. No dark mode, no neon, no glass blur, no gradients on UI.
- **Type**: Sentient light (display), Schibsted Grotesk (everything else). Small uppercase
  tracked eyebrows; no monospace, no pixel fonts.
- **3D**: flat-shaded, faceted, generated in code from primitives (no model files). One
  vertex-coloured Lambert material, soft sun shadows (re-rendered only when the sun moves), no
  post-processing. Detail comes from many small honest things (trails, fences, flowers, a
  woodpile, gulls, chimney smoke), never from textures. Sky and light drift from dawn to a pale
  dusk across the scroll and always stay light.
- **Motion**: the camera is the motion. UI motion is limited to a single fade-up on reveal.

### Design Principles
1. **Native scroll, always.** No scroll-jacking, no wheel hijack, no smooth-scroll library. The
   camera follows the page; the page never follows the camera.
2. **Text lives on a card.** Body copy never sits directly on the scene. The hero title is the one
   exception, set on open sky.
3. **Text and landmark stay in step.** Camera timing comes from each card's measured position
   (`src/journey/journeyStore.js`), not from a global scroll percentage.
4. **The text never waits on 3D.** HTML is prerendered; three.js is a lazy chunk imported only
   from `src/island/` (enforced by ESLint).
5. **Every layer degrades, content identical.** Reduced motion: the island stays but the camera
   cuts between stops. No WebGL, lost context or data saver: a still image over a sky gradient.
6. **One accent.** Coral marks the current stop, dates, links on hover, roofs and flags. Nothing
   else competes with it.
7. **Real content is sacred.** Facts live in `src/data/`; components only present them.
